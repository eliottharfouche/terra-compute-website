Switzerland runs one of the cleanest and most reliable grids in Europe

58.5% of Swiss electricity comes from hydropower, one of the lowest-carbon power systems in Europe.

At the centre of Europe's data centre corridor

Between Frankfurt, Milan and Paris, tied into the European grid by 41 cross-border lines.

The Swiss Alps are Europe's battery

Alpine reservoirs hold 8.9 TWh of power, ready for winter and for the peak hours of the European grid.

Our proprietary software makes grid capacity predictable

It analyses every parcel in Switzerland, finds the latent capacity at the substations that serve them and shows early which sites can be energised.
